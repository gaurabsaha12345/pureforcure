Sponsor Nutritional Kits for TB Patients/ Dry Ration Kits

Under the Pradhan Mantri TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (Ni-kshay Mitra) and community relief initiatives, Pure for Cure partners with leading corporate CSR bodies to provide monthly protein-rich nutritional food baskets to tuberculosis patients and wholesome dry ration kits to economically vulnerable families across Assam.

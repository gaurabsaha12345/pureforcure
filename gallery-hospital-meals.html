Hospital Caregiver Meals

Daily Sattvik Meals Distribution at Hospitals

Our flagship daily healthcare nutrition service across BBCI, GMCH, and AIIMS Guwahati, delivering over 500+ warm, nutritious Sattvik meals every single afternoon to devoted family caregivers.

  • 100% Fresh Sattvik Preparation (Zero Onion, Zero Garlic)
  • Served Piping Hot Directly to Families in Need
  • Under 80G Tax Exemption
  • 100% Real-Time Photographic Transparency

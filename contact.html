How to Reach Hare Krishna Movement

Located on the picturesque northern banks of the Brahmaputra River, near IIT Guwahati.

Campus Accessibility

The temple is easily accessible from all parts of Guwahati via multiple scenic and convenient routes:

Guwahati River Ropeway:

Board at Panbazar Ropeway Terminal to North Guwahati, then take a 5-minute e-rickshaw ride directly to the main gate.

Inland Waterways Ferry:

Take the regular passenger ferry service from Fancy Bazar / Uzan Bazar Ghat to Rajaduar / Madhyamkhanda Ghat.

By Road via Saraighat Bridge:

Drive across the Saraighat Bridge via Jalukbari toward Amingaon and follow signs for IIT Guwahati & Hare Krishna Movement Marg.

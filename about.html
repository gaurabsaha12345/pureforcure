Hare Krishna Movement, North Guwahati

A spiritual haven of peace, devotion, and Vedic culture situated on the pristine northern bank of the sacred Brahmaputra River.

Spiritual Mentorship & Origin

Hare Krishna Movement, Guwahati is a registered non-profit religious and charitable trust (spiritually mentored by ISKCON Bangalore). Our foundational vision is to cultivate a simpler, healthier, and more natural way of life through spiritual wisdom, cultural values, and selfless social welfare.

The Hare Krishna Movement was brought to the whole world in this modern age by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada (addressed with devotion as "Srila Prabhupada"), Founder-Acharya of the worldwide Hare Krishna Movement. By his deep devotion to his spiritual master, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura, and Lord Sri Krishna, he spread the sublime mission of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu worldwide.

Spreading the timeless message of peace through Harinama Sankirtan, distribution of Srila Prabhupada’s authentic Vedic literature, and free sanctified food (Sattvik Prasadam), Hare Krishna Movement Guwahati translates devotional prayer into active social compassion across Northeast India.

Pure for Cure: Nourishing Souls, Nurturing Lives

Pure for Cure was founded on the timeless vision of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada — Founder-Acharya of the Worldwide Hare Krishna Movement — whose directive was simple yet profound: "No one within a ten-mile radius of a temple should go hungry."

Based in Guwahati, Assam, we distribute freshly cooked, sanctified food (Sattvik Prasadam) to patients, hospital caregivers, orphaned children, the elderly, and underserved communities across India's North Eastern region — and growing.

Our Mission "No one should go hungry" — Srila Prabhupada

"To offer pure, sanctified food (Prasadam) with affection to patients, caregivers, underserved communities, and everyone in need at hospitals, orphanages, old age homes, slums, and wherever required — thereby addressing hunger and malnutrition while nurturing the spirit of selfless service."

TRANSCENDENTAL NUTRITION

Pure for Cure: What, Why & How?

Grounded in timeless Vedic culinary science and modern nutritional biochemistry. Learn why our food is pure, healing, and offered to Lord Sri Krishna.

Definition & Inviolable Principles

100% Lacto-Vegetarian Sattvik Nutrition

Pure for Cure is our non-negotiable dietary and healing philosophy. It defines food that is prepared in the highest mode of material nature – Sattva Guna (the mode of goodness, purity, and light).

Zero Onion, Zero Garlic & Zero Mushrooms: In Vedic science, alliums (onion/garlic) are classified under Rajas (passion) and Tamas (ignorance) because they agitate nervous energy and induce digestive heat. Our meals are 100% free from onion and garlic.
Pure Mustard Oil & Cold-Pressed Oils: Cooked strictly with pure mustard oil and cold-pressed mustard oil. We never reuse cooking oil, preventing trans fats and harmful oxidative free radicals.
Natural Digestive Herbs & Whole Spices: Infused with organic turmeric, fresh ginger root, cumin, hing (asafoetida), and coriander – ancient botanicals recognized for gut recovery and anti-inflammatory protection.

Core Ingredients in Every Service Meal

Grains & Pulses Assam Joha & Sona Masoori Rice, Yellow Moong Dal, Toor Dal
Fresh Vegetables Pumpkin, Carrots, Green Beans, Ridge Gourd, Fresh Spinach
Healthy Oils Pure Mustard Oil, Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil
Ayurvedic Seasoning Curry leaves, Rock salt (Saindhava), Fresh ginger, Turmeric
100% Certified Under Food Safety & Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

1. Vedic Science (Bhagavad Gita)

In the Bhagavad Gita (17.8), Lord Sri Krishna declares:

"Foods that are juicy, wholesome, comforting, and pleasant to the heart increase life duration, purify one's mind, and grant strength, health, happiness, and peace."

Sattvik food cleanses the consciousness, calming anxious minds and inspiring noble thoughts.

2. Medical Care for Caregivers

Hospital attendants endure chronic exhaustion, lack of sleep, and extreme psychological stress. Consuming spicy or oily street vendor food causes acidity, gastritis, and chronic illness.

Our gentle, alkaline Sattvik khichdi provides instant, easily assimilable carbohydrates, electrolytes, and vegetable antioxidants – keeping caregivers physically robust so they can support their loved ones.

3. Dignity & Compassionate Care

Hospital attendants frequently endure emotional distress, long waiting hours, and financial exhaustion. Serving sanctified, warm Sattvik meals restores hope and peace of mind during crisis.

Every plate is served with utmost dignity, warmth, and respect, ensuring no patient attendant in Assam feels isolated or abandoned during medical emergencies.

From Sacred Cauldron to Hospital Kiosk: A 5-Stage Journey

1

Daily Farm Sourcing

Hand-sorted pulses, unpolished rice, and fresh vegetables sourced early every morning from certified local farmers.

2

Automated Cooking

Cooked in 304 food-grade stainless steel steam cauldrons. Devotees chant sacred mantras to ensure high spiritual sanctity.

3

Offered to Sri Krishna

Offered with Tulasi leaves upon the temple altar with Vedic hymns, sanctifying every grain into transcendental Sattvik Meals.

4

65°C+ Hot Logistics

Sealed hot inside double-walled insulated stainless steel carriers maintaining food above 65°C throughout transport.

5

Dignified Distribution

Served at hospital kiosks in biodegradable bowls with folded hands, welcoming smiles, and pure brotherly compassion.

DIVINE PHILOSOPHY

The Philosophy of Pure for Cure
& Hare Krishna Movement

Grounded in Vedic wisdom, our philosophy synthesizes selfless social welfare with deep spiritual upliftment and compassionate community healthcare support.

1. Simple Living, High Thinking

Following timeless Vedic traditions, we advocate a harmonious lifestyle centered on natural sustainability, clean habits, inner purity, and devotion to God.

2. Sattvik Meals as Sacred Medicine

Food prepared with love and offered with gratitude to the Lord becomes Sattvik Meals. Pure for Cure brings mental peace, happiness & nourishes both body and soul.

3. Universal Compassion & healthcare support

Serving hospitalized families without discrimination. We channel volunteer dedication towards relieving pain, hunger, and vulnerability for patients and caregivers across Assam.

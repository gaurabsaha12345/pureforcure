Lives Nourished by Your Kindness

Behind every plate of Sattvik Meals is an exhausted family fighting for their loved one's survival. Here are four real journeys of hope.

BBCI Hospital Food Distribution, Guwahati
Bipul Das & Daughter Ananya Caregiver for 4 Months

A Lifeline of nutritious food When All Savings Went to Chemo

“I had to borrow money just to afford my daughter’s injections. I used to skip lunch and drink tap water to save ₹50 a day. When Pure for Cure gave me hot, pure sattvik meals, I broke down in tears.”

Bipul traveled 140 km from Goalpara to Guwahati after his 14-year-old daughter was diagnosed with acute leukemia. Having spent all his farming savings on diagnostics and medicine, he slept on cardboard in the hospital corridor. Pure for Cure's daily lunchtime distribution outside B. Borooah Cancer Institute (BBCI) provides Bipul and hundreds of cancer caregivers with freshly prepared, easy-to-digest Sattvik meals every single day.

AIIMS Guwahati Hospital Food Distribution Kiosk
Pranita Kalita Renal Caregiver from Barpeta

Dignity, Hygiene & Care for Elderly Dialysis Attendants

“AIIMS Guwahati Changsari is far outside Guwahati city. Food stalls outside were unhygienic and expensive. Pure for Cure’s kiosk is clean, peaceful, and serves food with genuine affection.”

Pranita escorts her 68-year-old husband twice a week from rural Barpeta for dialysis at AIIMS Guwahati. Being an elderly caregiver, finding affordable, light, nutritious food was a grueling struggle. Pure for Cure's permanent hygienic kiosk provides nourishing Sattvik khichdi, vegetable curry, and clean drinking water, giving attendants like Pranita the physical energy to manage critical hospital hours.

Morigaon Flood-Hit Villages
Hemanta Baruah & Family Emergency Disaster Relief

fresh khichdi Brought by Boat When Kitchens Were Submerged

“Our earthen chulha was under five feet of floodwater. For two days our little children ate wet puffed rice. When the temple food boat arrived with steaming khichdi, it was nothing short of divine intervention.”

During the catastrophic monsoon floods in Morigaon, over 80 villages were submerged and cut off from electricity and road transport. Pure for Cure mobilized disaster relief boat teams and heavy vehicle tractors, delivering freshly cooked, packaged fresh Sattvik meals to marooned families stranded on embankments and raised bamboo platforms.

Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) Outreach
Nurul Islam Trauma Ward Attendant from Darrang

Strength for Exhausted Overnight Attendants at Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH)

“My brother was admitted to Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) ICU after a highway crash. Running between pharmacy counters and diagnostic labs, I didn’t sleep or eat for 36 hours. The nutritious meal given by the volunteers gave me strength to stand.”

The Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) receives the heaviest emergency trauma load across the North-East. Anxious attendants often stand outside ICUs for consecutive days without shelter or nourishing food. Pure for Cure's mobile delivery vans distribute hot, nutritious Sattvik dinner trays directly outside the trauma and emergency blocks every evening.

Understanding the Need

The Hidden Crisis of Hospital Caregivers

While medical teams focus on patients, the caregivers who nurse them face unseen physical, emotional, and nutritional hardship.

What Caregivers Face Daily:
  • Severe Financial Exhaustion: Families spend entire life savings on medicines, chemotherapy, and ICU beds, leaving ₹0 for daily food.
  • Skipping Meals for Days: 72% of attendants outside government hospital wards skip at least one meal daily to save money.
  • Unhygienic Roadside Stalls: Attendants consume stale, oily roadside food, often falling sick themselves and delaying their patient's discharge.
  • Emotional Despair: Sleeping on cold hospital verandas and waiting anxiously outside ICUs causes immense mental trauma.
Pure for Cure's Holistic Solution:
  • Nutritious, Free / Subsidized Meals: Nourishing meals provided at ₹25 or entirely free, ensuring no attendant sleeps on an empty stomach.
  • Automated FSSAI Central Kitchen: Cooked in hygienic, stainless steel steam-jacketed cauldrons adhering to strict FSSAI safety norms.
  • Pure Sattvik Ingredients: Prepared without onion or garlic, rich in wholesome lentils, seasonal vegetables, and digestion-friendly spices.
  • Sanctified Spiritual Solace: Every vessel of food is offered with prayers to Lord Krishna, infusing peaceful spiritual vibrations into troubled minds.

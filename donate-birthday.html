Birthday & Anniversary Celebration through Annadan

Birthday & Milestone Annadan Seva

About this food initiative

Make your personal or family milestones immortal by distributing food to those who hunger in hospital corridors. In Indian culture, performing Annadan (sacred food distribution) on one's birthday, wedding anniversary, or parents' memorial day is the highest virtue. Pure for Cure allows you to sponsor an entire day's meal distribution in honor of your special day.

Highlights of this food initiative:

  • Dedicated prayer offered in your name at the temple before morning cooking
  • Sponsor 100, 200, or 500 meals dedicated to your loved one
  • Photo memory report sent to your email after the meal drive concludes
  • Official 80G tax exemption receipt for your family

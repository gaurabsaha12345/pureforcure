Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) Food Distribution

GMCH Guwahati Bhangagarh

About this food initiative

Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) in Bhangagarh handles thousands of acute emergencies, accident traumas, and pediatric admissions daily. With corridors crowded day and night, family attendants often sleep on floor mats with zero access to clean drinking water or affordable food. Pure for Cure rolls out daily meal distributions right outside casualty gates.

Highlights of this food initiative:

  • Over 600 fresh meals distributed during peak hospital lunch hours
  • Eco-friendly leaf and paper packaging ensuring zero hospital litter
  • Nutritionally dense blend of rice, lentils, and seasonal greens
  • Recognized and facilitated by Guwahati district hospital administration

The Reality of Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) Trauma & Emergency Corridors

High-Stress Trauma Center:

Guwahati Medical College & Hospital handles the heaviest trauma, burn, and pediatric referral caseload in North East India. Families rushed in from road accidents or sudden organ failure find themselves sleeping on hospital verandas with zero food or financial safety net.

Reliable Nourishment in Crisis:

When food vans arrive outside casualty, attendants do not need to choose between buying medicine or eating lunch. Our volunteer network serves 600+ fresh, nutritious meals every single day in eco-friendly leaf plates with care and deep respect.

Distribution Schedule: 12:00 PM- 2:00 PM (Daily except National Holidays)

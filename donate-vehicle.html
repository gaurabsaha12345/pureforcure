Insulated Food Transit Vans

About this food initiative

Cooking 3,000+ meals daily is only half the battle - transporting heavy insulated steam containers through Guwahati traffic and monsoon floods to AIIMS Guwahati, Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH), and BBCI requires reliable, customized food vehicles. Sponsoring vehicle acquisition, fuel, and upkeep ensures that no hospital attendant is ever left waiting because of transit breakdowns.

Highlights of this food initiative:

  • Custom insulated steel cargo bay maintaining 65°C nutritious food safety
  • Covers over 80 km daily across North Guwahati and south river banks
  • Equipped for quick response during Assam emergency flood relief operations
  • Donor acknowledgment plaque mounted on the vehicle

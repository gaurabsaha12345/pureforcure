Compassion in Action

Food Distribution for Those Who Wait By Loved Ones' Bedsides

When a patient is hospitalized in Guwahati from remote corners of Assam, family members camp outside corridors with little money and no cooking facilities. Canteen meals are often unaffordable, and attendants frequently skip food for days.

Pure for Cure operates hygienic food kiosks and mobile delivery vans at AIIMS Guwahati, Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH), and Dr. B. Borooah Cancer Institute (BBCI), serving wholesome, freshly cooked Sattvik Meals completely free of charge or at subsidized service rates.

Sattvik Food & Holistic Nutrition

Nourishing Body & Soul with Sattvik Meals

At Pure for Cure, we believe that healing begins from within. Sattvik food prepared with pure intent instills vital energy, immunity, and emotional strength.

100% Pure Vegetarian & Sattvik

Prepared without onion, garlic, or chemical additives in FSSAI-compliant facilities. Nourishing, gentle on digestion, and formulated for healing.

Healthy Habits for Vitality

Freshly cooked nourishing meals packed with plant proteins, wholesome grains, and healing spices that revitalize weary hospital caregivers.

Untouched & Mechanized Hygiene

Cooked at 65°C+ in automated kitchen setup as per FSSAI compliance and transported in insulated thermal food containers with strict zero-hand-touch protocols.

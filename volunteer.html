Volunteer & CSR Service
Why Volunteer With Us?

The Joy of Selfless Service

Volunteering at Pure for Cure is more than community service — it is an opportunity to uplift weary hearts and experience spiritual tranquility through Sattvik Meals service.

Direct On-Ground Impact

Hand-deliver freshly cooked nourishing meals directly to cancer caregivers and rural families waiting anxiously outside intensive care units.

Spiritual Peace & Purity

Immerse yourself in the sacred atmosphere of Hare Krishna Movement. Serving nutritious Sattvik meals brings profound inner peace and mental clarity.

Flexible Service Schedules

Whether you have 2 hours on a weekend morning or want to coordinate a weekday distribution drive, we offer customized volunteer shifts.

Certificate of Recognition

Receive an official Certificate of Volunteering & Service Appreciation from Hare Krishna Movement Guwahati, ideal for academic and professional portfolios.

Service Opportunities

Explore Open Volunteer Roles

Choose a role that resonates with your skills, availability, and location across Guwahati.

Hospital Outreach

Hospital Kiosk Distribution Volunteer

Serve nourishing meals with care and respect directly to attendants outside AIIMS Guwahati Changsari, Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH), or B. Borooah Cancer Institute (BBCI).

Locations: AIIMS Guwahati Changsari / Guwahati Medical College & Hospital (GMCH) / BBCI Guwahati
Timings: Lunch Shift (11:30 AM – 2:00 PM) or Dinner Shift (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM)
Responsibilities: Food distribution, queue assistance, sharing water and compassionate encouragement.
Apply for Kiosk Service
kitchen service

Central Automated kitchen service

Join our dedicated cooking and packaging team at the state-of-the-art automated central kitchen at North Guwahati.

Location: Hare Krishna Movement Central Kitchen, Ghorajan, North Guwahati
Timings: Morning Shift (6:30 AM – 9:30 AM)
Responsibilities: Vegetable preparation, grain sorting, packaging meal boxes, hygiene audits.
Apply for kitchen service
Emergency Response

Disaster & Flood Relief Emergency Taskforce

Be on standby during monsoon floods to deliver relief food kits, dry rations, and clean water to inundated villages across Assam.

Locations: Morigaon, Kamrup, Barpeta flood belts
Timings: On-call emergency deployments (Weekends & Seasonal)
Responsibilities: Relief van logistics, boat distribution, kit packaging, community coordination.
Join Relief Taskforce

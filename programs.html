DAILY SEVA & ON-GOING MISSIONS

Pure For Cure On-going Initiatives

From premier medical institutions to auspicious family celebrations and sacred Amavasya Annadanam, our daily missions deliver pure, hygienic Sattvik meals with devotion and care.

CANCER CARE MEALS

BBCI Hospital Food Distribution

Patients and their attendants camping outside Dr. B. Borooah Cancer Institute face intense physical and emotional trauma. Pure for Cure runs a dedicated food kiosk and daily meals distribution ensuring 400+ patients and families receive comforting, nutritious, digestible Sattvik meals.

Daily Cancer Care

AIIMS CAREGIVER SEVA

AIIMS Hospital Food Distribution

Located in Changsari across the Brahmaputra, AIIMS Guwahati has hundreds of caregivers traveling long distances. Pure for Cure operates a dedicated distribution kiosk providing hot lunch meals daily to attendants who have no local kitchen or affordable canteen access.

500+ Daily Caregivers

GMCH TRAUMA SEVA

GMCH Hospital Food Distribution

As the largest referral hospital in the region, GMCH handles acute emergency, casualty, and pediatric cases daily. Pure for Cure serves over 600+ nutritious, hot, hygienically packed meals every single day to caregivers waiting vigilantly beside the hospital.

DISASTER RELIEF & SPECIAL MISSIONS

Pure For Cure Recent Initiatives

Frontline disaster responses, pandemic emergency relief, mass cultural pilgrim feeding, and rural tribal development drives conducted with speed, scale, and uncompromising quality.

DISASTER RESPONSE

Assam Flood Relief

When monsoon floods submerge thousands of villages across Assam, Pure for Cure deploys motorized rescue boats, setting up mobile cooking camps to deliver fresh, piping hot meals and safe water directly to marooned families stranded on embankments and relief camps.

1,00,000+ Meals Served

PANDEMIC RELIEF

COVID-19 Food Relief

During peak lockdown phases, our centralized kitchen operated continuously with strict safety standards, delivering over 4,00,000 cooked meals and distributing essential ration kits to stranded migrant workers, daily wage laborers, and healthcare personnel across Assam.

4,00,000+ Meals View Archive
SACRED RIVER FESTIVAL

Namami Brahmaputra

During the historic river festival celebrated across Assam, Pure for Cure arranged large-scale daily Mahabhog distribution along the riverfront, serving tens of thousands of pilgrims, sadhus, and visitors with divine Sattvik feast prasadam.

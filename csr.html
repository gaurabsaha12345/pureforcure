Esteemed CSR Partners & Sponsorships

Leading public sector and corporate enterprises empowering Pure for Cure to provide daily hot, nutritious meals to thousands of hospital caregivers across Assam.

CSR Kiosk Partner BBCI Guwahati

Lohia Group

Under their dedicated Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) healthcare mandate, Lohia Group has sponsored and established the permanent hospital food distribution kiosk outside Dr. B. Borooah Cancer Institute (BBCI), Guwahati. This noble partnership ensures patient attendants and cancer fighters receive soothing, nutritious, freshly prepared Sattvik meals daily without financial distress.

Supported: BBCI Cancer Hospital Kiosk Sponsorship & Daily Meal Drives
Premier Healthcare CSR
CSR Kiosk Partner AIIMS Guwahati

Avant Garde

Avant Garde has championed caregiver healthcare nutrition under its visionary CSR programme by sponsoring the permanent hospital food distribution kiosk outside AIIMS Guwahati, Changsari. This impactful contribution ensures hundreds of patient attendants arriving from remote North-East districts receive hot, hygienic Sattvik meals daily with dignity.

Supported: AIIMS Guwahati Hospital Food Kiosk Adoption & Caregiver Annadanam

GAIL (India) Limited

Under their flagship Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) healthcare mandate, GAIL (India) Limited partners with Pure for Cure to sponsor hot, nourishing Sattvik meals for patient attendants camping outside major healthcare hubs in Guwahati. GAIL's compassionate support ensures destitute families receive daily nutrition with dignity during demanding medical treatments.

Supported: Hospital Food Kiosk Adoption & Caregiver Nutrition Drives

Numaligarh Refinery Limited (NRL)

Numaligarh Refinery Limited (NRL) has championed Pure for Cure's food logistics and delivery infrastructure across Assam through its visionary CSR programme. NRL's contribution has empowered our mobile insulated food vans to transport 3,000+ fresh Sattvik meals daily from our central kitchen to remote hospital wards and relief distribution points.
